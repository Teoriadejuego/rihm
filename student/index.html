How to read this experiment

Indicators overlap

Altruist, egalitarian, selfish and antisocial are overlapping indicators of decision patterns. A consistent response can match more than one, so their percentages can exceed 100% when added.

These labels describe choices in this task, not personality or moral worth.

Two denominators

The four social indicators and the compassion–envy matrix use consistent responses. Inconsistent uses complete binary responses. Incomplete and non-binary responses are excluded from public calculations.

Protected aggregates

Protected means a value was withheld before publication. Small counts, small denominators and small complements are protected; related cells may also be withheld. A protected value is never zero.

Filters select published aggregates. No individual answers are sent to this page.
